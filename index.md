# Bullet Rush Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 30 September 2026

Bullet Rush ("the game") is a mobile arena shooter for Android, published by **ManGoBaby**
("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play, who
collects it, and the choices you have.

## In short

- The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We never ask for your name, email,
  contacts, photos or location.
- Your progress and settings are saved only on your phone. We have no servers and receive
  no copy of them.
- The game shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Google collects some device information to
  show and measure those ads, as described below.

## Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, experience, unlocked items and weapons, cosmetics) and
your settings (graphics quality) in the app's private storage on your phone. This
data stays on your device: it is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you
uninstall the game or clear its data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show:

- **rewarded ads**, only when you choose to watch one (for example to triple a reward), and
- occasional **full-screen ads** between matches.

To serve ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, Google may
collect and process the following from your device:

- device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
- your IP address, and the approximate location that can be derived from it;
- device and app information (device model, Android version, language, app version);
- ad interactions (which ads were shown, taps, whether a rewarded ad was watched to the
  end);
- diagnostic data about the ads SDK (crashes and performance).

This information is collected directly by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit.
Google uses it as described in:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

Whether the ads are personalised depends on your consent and on the law where you live
(see below).

## Your choices

- **Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland):** when you first
  open the game, Google's consent form asks whether ads may use your data for
  personalisation. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from
  **Settings > Privacy options** in the game (shown in the regions where consent is
  required).
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings
  (**Settings > Privacy > Ads**, or **Settings > Google > Ads** on some phones). Once it is
  deleted, apps cannot use it for personalised ads.
- **Deleting your data:** uninstalling the game, or **Settings > Apps > Bullet Rush >
  Storage > Clear data**, removes everything the game saved on your phone. For the data
  Google holds, use [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com) and the controls in
  Google's Privacy Policy.

## Permissions

The game does not ask you to grant any permissions. Its package declares internet and
network-state access (to load ads) and the advertising-ID permission; the Google ads SDK
adds a few background permissions it needs to work (wake lock, foreground service, basic
phone state). The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise
location.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information
through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective
date.
